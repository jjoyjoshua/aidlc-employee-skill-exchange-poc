## Business Requirements Document

## Project: Employee Skill Exchange Portal

## 1. Project Overview

Our organization would like to develop an Employee Skill Exchange Portal that enables employees to share their knowledge and learn new skills from one another. The goal is to encourage continuous learning, improve collaboration across departments, and make better use of the expertise that already exists within the organization.

## 2. Business Need

Many organizations have employees with valuable knowledge and skills, but there is no centralized platform to identify and share that expertise. As a result:

- Employees often do not know who has expertise in a particular skill.

- Organizations spend money on external training even when internal experts are available.

- Knowledge remains within individual departments instead of being shared across the organization.

- Employees have limited opportunities for continuous learning and career growth.

To address these challenges, the organization requires an Employee Skill Exchange Portal that provides a centralized platform for employees to teach, learn, and collaborate with one another.

## 3. Business Objectives

The proposed solution aims to:

- Encourage knowledge sharing across the organization.

- Reduce dependency on external training providers.

- Promote continuous employee learning and development.

- Improve collaboration between departments.

- Identify and utilize internal subject matter experts.

- Increase employee engagement and participation.

- Preserve organizational knowledge.


- Build a strong learning culture.

## 4. Business Value

Implementing this solution will help the organization:

- Improve employee productivity and skill development.

- Reduce external training costs.

- Increase collaboration across departments.

- Maximize the use of internal talent.

- Improve employee engagement and satisfaction.

- Support continuous learning and innovation.

## Application Flow

## 1. User Login

I want employees to be able to securely log in using their company credentials. Only authorized employees should have access to the portal.

## Requirements:

- Employees should log in using their corporate email address and password.

- The system should validate the user's credentials before granting access.

- Employees should be able to reset their password if they forget it.

- Users should be automatically redirected to their dashboard after a successful login.

## 2. Employee Dashboard

After logging in, I want employees to see a dashboard that provides an overview of their learning activities.

The dashboard should display:

- Upcoming training sessions.

- Pending booking requests.

- Skills they are teaching.


- Skills they are currently learning.

- Recent notifications and announcements.

## 3. Employee Profile

I want every employee to have a profile where they can maintain their professional information.

Employees should be able to:

- View and update their profile.

- Add a short professional description.

- Update their department and designation if required.

- Upload a profile picture.

## 4. Skill Management

I want employees to list the skills they are willing to teach as well as the skills they want to learn.

Employees should be able to:

- Add new skills.

- Edit existing skills.

- Remove skills that are no longer relevant.

- Specify their proficiency level for each skill.

## 5. Skill Search

I want employees to search for colleagues who can teach specific skills.

The search should allow employees to filter results based on:

- Skill name.

- Department.

- Skill level.

- Trainer ratings.


## 6. Session Booking

I want employees to book training sessions with available trainers.

The system should allow employees to:

- Select a trainer.

- Choose an available date and time.

- Submit a booking request.

- View the status of their booking.

## 7. Booking Approval

I want trainers to review incoming booking requests and either approve or reject them.

Employees should be informed of the trainer's decision through notifications.

## 8. Notifications

I want the system to notify employees whenever there is an important update.

Examples include:

- Booking confirmation.

- Booking approval or rejection.

- Session reminders.

- Feedback requests after a completed session.

## 9. Session Completion

After a session is completed, I want the trainer to mark the session as completed.

Once completed, the learner should be prompted to provide feedback.


## 10. Feedback

I want learners to rate the trainer and provide comments after every completed session.

The feedback should help other employees identify highly rated trainers.

## 11. Learning History

I want employees to have access to a complete history of the skills they have learned.

The history should include:

- Skill name.

- Trainer.

- Date completed.

- Certificate.

- Feedback submitted.

## 12. Teaching History

I also want employees who act as trainers to view a history of the sessions they have conducted.

The history should include:

- Learner name.

- Skill taught.

- Session date.

- Rating received.


## 13. Logout

I want employees to be able to securely log out of the system.

Once logged out, they should no longer be able to access the portal without logging in again.

## Expected Outcome

I expect this portal to help employees share knowledge internally, improve cross-functional collaboration, reduce external training costs, and encourage continuous professional development. The solution should be user-friendly, secure, responsive, and scalable so it can support our organization's future growth.
