Avatar picker

The profile picture control on SCR-005 (REQ-007). It saves on its own, outside the profile form's single Save: an upload takes a different amount of time and fails in different ways from four short text fields, and bundling the two would make one Save that can half-succeed.

ST-12 — That photo can't be used

JJ
⚠ That file is 4.2 MB. Please choose a JPG, PNG or WebP under 2 MB.

The avatar reverts to exactly what it was before the attempt, and the error names the file's actual size and the actual rule. A generic "invalid file" is unactionable — the employee cannot tell whether to resize, convert or give up. The same line covers a failed upload, with its own wording.
