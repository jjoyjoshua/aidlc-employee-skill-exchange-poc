Confirm dialog

The modal confirm for an action that costs somebody else something. On SCR-008 it is cancelling a confirmed session (REQ-020): a time a colleague committed to disappears, and they are notified (REQ-028). Actions that only affect the person taking them confirm in the row instead — see availability-row's Remove on SCR-007. That line is the whole reason this component exists.

The confirm step

Cancel this session?

Excel with Priya M., today at 14:00.

Priya will be notified. This can’t be undone.

Two lines and no more: what is being cancelled, named exactly, and what follows. Nothing is promised about what happens to Priya’s 14:00 afterwards, because no requirement says — that is conflict C-1.

The second dialog is the special case: the other party cancelled while this one was open. One action, not two — a retry that will fail identically is not an offer. Closing it leaves the row showing “Cancelled by Priya M.” rather than restoring it.
